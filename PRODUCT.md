# PRODUCT.md

## What this is

The personal research portfolio and GitHub profile of Satheesh Meadi, PhD student in Biosystems and Agricultural Engineering at Oklahoma State University (RISE Lab). One static page, published with GitHub Pages from `main`; the README of this repository doubles as the GitHub profile.

## Who reads it

Faculty, collaborators, conference contacts, recruiters and growers who met Satheesh in the field. They arrive from LinkedIn, a conference slide, the GitHub profile or a direct link. Most read on a laptop in an office; a meaningful share open it on a phone right after meeting him.

## What it must prove

That he does both halves of the work: the field work (installing and maintaining sensors, visiting growers) and the modelling (canopy temperature estimation, humidity constraints, OpenET-based irrigation estimation), plus the skills and service that go with them. Evidence over claims: dated visits, real photographs, listed papers and talks.

## Brand commitments

- OSU orange `#fe5c00` is the single accent (https://brand.okstate.edu).
- Tagline: "Intelligence, rooted in the field."
- Section anchors (`#research`, `#fieldwork`, `#gallery`, `#projects`, `#publications`, `#about`, `#contact`) are stable links used elsewhere.
- Every factual claim traces to the CV or dated field notes; nothing is invented or rounded for effect.

## Out of scope

Blog, CMS, analytics, comments. No tracking scripts.
