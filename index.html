    I’m Satheesh Meadi, a PhD student in Biosystems and Agricultural Engineering at Oklahoma State University, working in the RISE Lab.

    My path began in computer science, continued through a master’s in data science at UMBC, and brought me to the field. Today I work across canopy temperature, irrigation estimation and the data systems that make research possible.

    I enjoy the whole process: wiring a sensor, questioning a measurement, building a model, and explaining what it means to someone who can use it.
